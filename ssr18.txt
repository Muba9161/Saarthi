Description
The Petrol Pumps API helps users discover and explore petrol pump stations across India with location-based search and live fuel pricing data.
It covers all major fuel companies — IOCL, HPCL, BPCL, Shell, Jio-BP, Nayara, and more — and combines GPS-based proximity search with real-time petrol, diesel, and CNG prices for each station.
📍 Finds nearby pumps by GPS coordinates within a defined radius
⛽ Filters stations by fuel type — petrol, diesel, or CNG-only
🏢 Supports company-based filtering with brand aliases (e.g. HP/HPCL, JIO/Jio-bp)
🕐 Identifies 24-hour open stations for highway or late-night travel
💰 Returns live fuel prices in INR/litre for petrol, diesel, and CNG
🗺️ Delivers map-ready coordinates with direction links for each pump
👉 This allows users to not just locate the nearest pump, but make smarter choices — comparing fuel prices across companies, finding open stations at any hour, or specifically hunting for CNG pumps in their area.
